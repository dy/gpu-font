    How it works

    The crop becomes up to three grayscale text windows. A small convolutional network scores each window; their scores are averaged to rank the fonts. The model runs locally on WebGPU or CPU.

    Use one regular font on a plain background. Weight and italics are not detected. Short crops and fonts outside the catalog can produce misleading matches.
